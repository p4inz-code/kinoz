## Why this is consistent
The bundled libmpv / FFmpeg build is GPL, and kinoz is distributed under GPL v3, which is compatible with GPL v2-or-later components. Qt is linked dynamically under LGPL v3, so you can replace the Qt libraries with your own build. The fonts' OFL license allows bundling and redistribution as long as the license text and copyright notice travel with them, which they do.

## Corresponding source (GPL section 6)
